PATINA — PRESS FACT SHEET

Where Time Adds Value.

Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison, Wisconsin. It is
built for the year a design studio brings on its first designer or
assistant and its workload doubles: one living document per client, from
brief to install. The studio's client sees the same page the designer
does, so there is one agreed direction.

The app and marketplace are pre-launch. The website introduces the
Living Study and invites studios to apply to the Founding 50. It does not
offer marketplace purchasing or a public app download.
