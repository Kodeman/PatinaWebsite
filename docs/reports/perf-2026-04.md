## SEO Δ

The May 2026 audit (run 2026-05-01) is the first month-over-month baseline. No prior audit to delta against, but the audit surfaced two critical issues that have plausibly been suppressing organic discovery throughout April:

1. **Production sitemap and `robots.txt` Sitemap directive contain literal newlines inside `<loc>` tags.** Search engines treat the malformed URLs as broken and skip them. Estimated affected URLs: ~24.
2. **Canonical host is `patina-website.vercel.app`, not `patina.cloud`.** Every `og:url`, sitemap entry, and JSON-LD `url` field points at the Vercel preview domain. Search authority is being built for the wrong hostname.

Both fix in `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` env var settings on Vercel + a small code change in `src/lib/seo.ts:4` and `src/app/robots.ts:3`. Per the audit's "Top 3 Quick Wins."

- All PostHog numbers come from HogQL queries against the `events` table for the date range `2026-04-01 00:00:00 UTC` to `2026-05-01 00:00:00 UTC`.
