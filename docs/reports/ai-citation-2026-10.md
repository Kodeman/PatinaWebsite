## Competitive Landscape

Big-box retail still owns query 4, Coohom/3dcloud/freeappsforme still feed queries 1 and 8, Oblist still feeds query 3, and generic measurement content still owns query 7. Four things moved.

**1. First Chair turned self-publishing into a summary mention in one month.** In September it showed up as a cited *source* on query 1 ("AI Furniture Visualizer Apps in 2026"). This month it has a second post ("Best AI Furniture Finder Apps in 2026"), which is cited on both queries 1 and 8. On query 8 the summary now names First Chair outright: "upload a room, share inspiration, or describe what you want, then get a complete concept built from real, shoppable pieces." Per `seo-audit-2026-10.md` it also self-publishes on "Havenly alternatives 2026." That's the clearest evidence yet that this panel rewards the self-publish play. It's also the closest thing on any query to Patina's original consumer pitch.

**2. App-store listings are now cited directly.** On query 2, 4 of 9 sources are App Store pages. On query 4, 3 of 9 are store pages (2 Google Play, 1 App Store). September's report recorded no store listings on either query. If this holds, the room-scanning and AR queries are partly decided by the listing itself. Patina is pre-launch and has no listing. And the brand-name collision on the App Store (*Patina — The Resale Wizard*, plus `patina-app.com` per the October SEO audit) applies on exactly this surface.

**3. Query 2 has a direct scan-and-shop competitor.** Roomform: "scan your room in 3D, capture furniture photos … redesign, shop, and visualize — all in perfect scale." September's read was that query 2 was drifting toward pure scanning tools (Polycam, a RoomPlan app). This month the summary still leans that way (Magicplan, RoomScan Pro), but it also names one app that sits exactly where furniture-shopping and room-scanning meet.

**4. Query 5 shifted toward tools for designers.** For three months the summary was Havenly / Houzz / Curate by Sotheby's. This month it's Houzz, Havenly, DesignFiles (all-in-one studio software) and Houzz Pro (leads, proposals, payments). That's the first time this panel has surfaced *studio-side* software. It matters because the current vision (`patina-merged/docs/vision/VISION.md`) defines Patina's customer as the growing design studio, not the homeowner.

**Other movement:**
- **Query 3:** Formed & Furnished rotated out again (third change of state in three months). Oblist strengthened its hold: it now supplies a third of the query's sources, including its "Ranked for 2026" roundup. CURADA held its position from September.
- **Query 6:** the AI-render tier (Remodel AI, Interior AI, RoomGPT) now appears in the summary itself, not only as sources. Aggregator pages (CB Insights, SaaSHub, Comparably) take 4 of 9 slots.
- **Query 7:** slightly healthier. Spam is down to one slot, and two tool-shaped results are present (ItemFits back, MeltFlex AI new). Still structurally closed to a pre-launch app.

**Competitor-authored roundups cited this month** (a brand publishing a "best of" or guide on its own domain): GlamAR, Coohom, Oblist, First Chair, DecorViz, Remodel AI, MeltFlex AI, Shapr3D. That's eight. None mention Patina.

## Hypotheses

1. **The content freeze is still the binding constraint, and this month has a cleaner control case.** First Chair published two roundups and earned a named summary mention within about a month. Patina published nothing and stayed at 0/8. The technical foundation is now mostly fixed, so the remaining gap between those two outcomes is content.

2. **This panel may be measuring the wrong customer.** All 8 queries were written in April for homeowners. Since then the vision has narrowed the customer to the design studio (raised as a blocker in `weekly-content-2026-09-28.md`). Of the 8 queries, only query 5 now points toward studio software, and this month it did move that way. Even if Patina published, the panel as written is mostly tracking a market Patina has said it isn't serving for the next 12 months. This is a measurement question, not a performance one. The 0/8 stands either way.

3. **Part of the room-scan and AR surface is closed until launch.** With store listings now cited directly on queries 2 and 4, an owned article can only compete for the remaining slots on those queries. This is one month of data. Confirm next month before acting on it.

4. **Query 3 is still the most winnable homeowner query, but now it's Oblist's to lose.** Formed & Furnished keeps rotating in and out, which suggests the summary layer has no settled view of "designer-curated, new." Meanwhile Oblist's ranked 2026 roundup gives it the anchor position. A Patina comparison post would compete against an incumbent roundup, not an empty slot.

5. **The coined term is still uncontested.** In the exploratory query "designer-taught furniture recommendations," nothing uses the phrase the way Patina does. Results are about design schools, self-taught designers and brand recommendations. This matches the October SEO audit's finding. It's the one query shape where Patina would be competing against no one.
