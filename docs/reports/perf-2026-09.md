## Appendix — Methodology

- **PostHog:** HogQL via `POST https://us.posthog.com/api/projects/326191/query/`, window `2026-09-01T00:00Z` → `2026-10-01T00:00Z`. The marketing filter is `properties.$host IN ('patina.cloud','www.patina.cloud','patina-website.kody-be3.workers.dev')`. This replaces June's path allowlist, now that every surface has its own host. Portal surfaces are filtered by their own hosts.
- **Consent gate:** `posthog.init(... opt_out_capturing_by_default: dnt || consent !== "granted")` in `src/lib/posthog.ts`. Because capture is blocked until opt-in, every marketing person counted had opted in by construction. Visitors who don't opt in aren't recorded.
- **Signup dedupe:** `founding_circle_signup` is captured both client-side and server-side, so signups are counted as distinct persons.
- **Payments:** `pay_link_payment_completed` `amount_cents` = 100 and 4200, both geolocated to Milwaukee. They're treated as probable QA. Livemode isn't on the event, so it's unverified.
- **Not queried:** Supabase `waitlist` (MCP declined: no approver), Sanity (MCP declined: no approver). Content counts come from `weekly-content-2026-09-28.md`, `seo-audit-2026-10.md` and `ai-citation-2026-10.md`.
- **Uncommitted paths:** `git status --short | wc -l` = 148 in `PatinaWebsite` at this run.
