## Competitive Landscape

The cited set is broadly stable versus June — the same tier-1 players anchor the same queries for the third month running. Five observations on what shifted:

**New adjacent entrant on query 3: Formed & Furnished.** This is the most notable change this month. Their AI-summary description — "designer video walkthroughs, 3 layout options per room size, direct product links, access to an AI design assistant, and a free 30-minute 1-on-1 consult with an interior designer" — occupies ground close to Patina's own positioning: curated + designer-connected + AI-assisted. They are approaching the "designer-curated + room-aware" intersection from the services side. Worth tracking as a direct-adjacent competitor, not just another marketplace.

**The Oblist's self-publish playbook persists.** Their "Best Curated Furniture Marketplaces Ranked for 2026" post is again the primary source AI summaries quote for query 3 — same as June. They published the canonical ranking; the summary layer quotes it; they sit at the top of their own list. Unchanged and hardening.

**Coohom is now doing the same thing.** Coohom's own articles ("Which Furniture Shopping Apps Are Worth Using in 2026?", "App to Identify Furniture From a Picture") are cited as sources on queries 1 and 8 this month. A second competitor is now self-publishing the roundups the AI layer quotes.

**Wayfair Decorify's language is still colonizing the category description.** "Like having a designer in your pocket — upload a photo, pick a style, AI fills in the blanks with a full makeover" appears verbatim across queries 1 and 8 again. This framing continues to harden as the default description for the AI-furniture-app category, and it overlaps with how Patina would want to be described.

**Apartment Therapy remains an active, reachable citation source.** It surfaces on queries 2 and 5 this month (as it did in June). It is a live source the summary layer pulls from — and still contains no Patina mention. Query 4 remains locked by big-box retailers (IKEA, Amazon, Wayfair, Target, Home Depot); query 7 remains generic measurement-guide content that is structurally hard for any app to enter.

**Still no platform owns "designer-curated + room-aware + AR."** Same gap as May and June. The territory remains open in AI summaries — but Formed & Furnished is now the first mover approaching it, and the window is no longer uncontested.

## Hypotheses

Grounded in this month's search results plus the content and SEO logs:

1. **The dominant blocker is execution, not lag.** The content the visibility plan is built on has not shipped. Per the weekly-content log, the journal has been static since April 7 and Tier 1/Tier 3 topics are at −9. Zero new AI-citation surfaces have been created since the plan began. This is now the leading explanation for a flat line — above indexing lag, entity ambiguity, or domain age.

2. **Listicle outreach still appears not to have happened.** The same third-party roundup domains recur every month (cgifurniture, freeappsforme, postindustria, coohom, oblist). None mention Patina. This was the #1 recommendation in both May and June. If outreach had landed, we would expect at least one of these recurring sources to name Patina by now.

3. **Entity and on-page basics remain open.** Per `seo-audit-2026-07.md`: the homepage title still omits `app`/`AR`/`room scan` keywords; inner-page Twitter cards fall back to homepage defaults; designers/makers have no `Person`/`Organization` schema; there is no evidence of a live Wikidata entry. These are prerequisites for AI-summary inclusion that remain unbuilt.

4. **Competitors are compounding while our surface is static.** The Oblist and Coohom self-publish the ranking posts the AI layer quotes; Formed & Furnished has entered adjacent territory. Every month of no new content is a month competitors extend their citation footprint uncontested.

5. **The positioning gap is real but no longer uncontested.** "Designer-curated + room-aware + AR" is still unclaimed in AI summaries, which is the opportunity. Formed & Furnished is the first entrant to approach it. The territory is open, but the first-mover window is narrowing.

## Methodology Notes

- WebSearch returns top organic + AI-summary content. We treat this as a proxy for what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude's browsing layers cite, since they index from the same source pool. This proxy under-detects citations that appear *only* in ungrounded conversational responses.
- "Patina" filter: a citation counts only if the result references patina.cloud or unambiguously refers to Patina the furniture platform. Patina restaurant group, patina.net, finish-tutorial pages, and the generic noun are excluded. No Patina reference of any kind appeared in the top results for any of the eight queries this month.
- Same eight queries and scoring rules as May and June, for continuity.
- Next run: 2026-08-02. If content has shipped in the interim, watch for first movement on queries 3 and 5 (softest fields) and any of the recurring roundup sources beginning to name Patina.
