## Competitive Landscape

Tier-1 anchors unchanged for a fifth month: big-box retail owns query 4, Havenly/Houzz/Curate own query 5, Coohom/cgifurniture/freeappsforme/PostIndustria feed queries 1 and 8, Oblist feeds query 3, generic measurement content owns query 7. What moved:

**Formed & Furnished returned to query 3 — and brought company.** August's disappearance was a one-month rotation, not an exit. This month it's back in the summary with its own domain cited, and a second designer-curated-package platform, CURADA (celebrity-designer shoppable rooms), appeared alongside it. The "designer-curated" territory that was uncontested in August now has two named occupants, both positioned on curation-of-new rather than vintage/resale. This is the closest any competitor has come to Patina's positioning, and it now looks durable rather than transient.

**The self-publish playbook added two more practitioners on query 1.** House AI ("The 8 Best Interior Design Apps in 2026" on its own blog, listing itself first) and First Chair ("AI Furniture Visualizer Apps in 2026") both entered as cited sources. With Oblist, Coohom, DecorViz, Remodel AI, House AI, and First Chair, six competitors have now run the same play: publish the roundup the AI layer quotes, on your own domain, with yourself in it. None mention Patina.

**Query 6 is consolidating around a new aggregator.** interiorinsider.com supplies two of the eight sources ("22 Brands Like Havenly" and a Havenly-vs-Modsy-vs-Decorist comparison dated 2026). Remodel AI's Modsy-shutdown post persists from August. Between them, the query's citation surface is now roughly half owned by two domains that didn't appear in July. Stuccco (3D rendering + designer) is a new named alternative; Spacejoy is back.

**Query 2 is drifting toward pure scanning tools.** Polycam and a standalone RoomPlan app entered the summary. The query is being read increasingly as "room scanner app" rather than "furniture app that scans rooms" — the furniture-plus-scan intersection (Patina's) is not what the summary layer is currently rewarding.

**Query 7 is degrading.** Two of eight results are spam pages hosted on a compromised .edu subdomain; ItemFits (August's only tool-shaped result) is gone. The query is both structurally closed and getting noisier — lowest priority, unchanged.

**Query 5 is stable and closed.** Same three platforms, third month. Source rotation happened (Decorilla out; bitcot, BDI, U.S. News in) without changing the summary. Entry would require appearing in DesignFiles' roundup or an equivalent recurring source.

## Hypotheses

Grounded in this month's results plus `seo-audit-2026-09.md` and `weekly-content-2026-08-31.md`:

1. **Content freeze is now the sole dominant blocker.** August's two-layer diagnosis (nothing written + nothing deployed) is down to one layer: the deploy shipped. But the journal enters month six at 3 posts (all April 3–7, all Tier-2), zero Tier-1, zero Tier-3, zero drafts. Every cited source on every query this month is an article; Patina has published nothing citable in 146 days. The output can't move until the input does.

2. **The "designer-curated" window is closing faster than assumed.** August's report called query 3 "uncontested again." One month later it has two named occupants with their own cited domains. The lesson from Remodel AI on query 6 (one aimed post claims a vacuum within a month) is now being repeated against Patina's own positioning. A Patina "designer-curated furniture platforms 2026" post is still differentiated — room-aware, designer-taught, curated-new — but it's now a comparison against two direct competitors, not a lonely claim.

3. **Self-publishing is the category norm, not an edge.** Six competitors now run it. The marginal advantage of the play is shrinking, but not running it means being the only platform in the category with no owned roundup for the summary layer to quote.

4. **Outreach targets are getting easier, not harder.** The recurring sources are increasingly small self-publishers (house AI, first chair, interiorinsider, 92interiors, remodelai, themotiontree) rather than Wayfair-scale editorial. These are the domains that respond to a pitch. Five months as a standing recommendation, still no evidence of a single outreach email.

5. **Remaining entity gaps still tax everything.** Per the September SEO audit: `og:image` dropped on 8 routes (code defect, ~20 min fix), `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` still wrong in Vercel (month five), `og-image.jpg`/`logo.png` still 404 (month four), no FAQ/ItemList schema, plus a newly discovered App Store name collision ("Patina — The Resale Wizard"). Even a published post would ship with a broken social card and no rich-result eligibility.
