## Competitive Landscape

The tier-1 anchors are unchanged for a fourth month: big-box retail owns query 4, Havenly/Houzz own query 5, Coohom/cgifurniture/freeappsforme feed queries 1 and 8, Oblist feeds query 3. What moved:

**Formed & Furnished dropped off query 3.** July's most notable new entrant — the first competitor approaching Patina's "designer-curated + room-aware" intersection — does not appear in this month's summary. The query field rotated toward vintage/resale marketplaces (Pamono, 1stDibs, Curiouz, Chairish) instead. The direct-adjacent threat receded, at least in this month's sample; the intersection is unclaimed again.

**The Oblist doubled its citation footprint on query 3.** Two Oblist posts are now cited sources for the same query (the 2026 ranking plus a "Websites Like Chairish" guide). The self-publish playbook isn't just persisting — it's compounding. One domain now supplies two of the five sources the summary layer draws from.

**Modsy's shutdown is reshaping query 6.** Results now explicitly frame the query as "where to go after the shutdown," and Remodel AI — an AI-redesign tool — published the post that owns that framing and named itself the answer. A category incumbent's exit created a citation vacuum, and a new entrant claimed it within the sample window. This is the clearest live demonstration yet of the mechanism the visibility plan is built on.

**A third and fourth self-publisher have appeared.** After Oblist and Coohom, this month adds DecorViz (query 1, own-domain 2026 roundup) and Remodel AI (query 6). Self-publishing the roundup the AI layer quotes is now the standard playbook in this category, executed by four competitors, none of which mention Patina.

**Query 5's summary narrowed.** From five platforms in July to three (Havenly, Houzz, Curate by Sotheby's). A narrower field is harder to enter but also indicates the summary layer is consolidating around fewer, stronger sources — inclusion in one of the recurring source articles (DesignFiles, Decorilla) matters more than before.

**Queries 4 and 7 remain structurally closed** — big-box retail and generic measurement content respectively. No change in four months; these are the lowest-priority targets.

## Hypotheses

Grounded in this month's results plus `weekly-content-2026-07-28.md` and `seo-audit-2026-08.md`:

1. **Execution remains the dominant blocker — and it's now two-layered.** The journal enters month five frozen at 3 posts (all April 3–7; zero Tier-1, zero Tier-3 — the formats AI systems cite). Worse, the work that *did* get done isn't live: the July 10 commits (title-doubling fix, messaging unification) exist only in git, while production serves a build from ≤ June 23. Nothing new has entered the indexing pipeline in four months, and even shipped code isn't deployed.

2. **The Modsy vacuum validates the strategy while the window is being taken.** Remodel AI went from absent to "named the closest replacement" on query 6 by publishing one well-aimed post into a shutdown vacuum. The mechanism the visibility plan bets on demonstrably works, on these exact queries, on this timescale — it's simply being executed by competitors.

3. **Listicle outreach — four months as a top rec, still no evidence of it.** The recurring source domains (coohom, cgifurniture, freeappsforme, oblist, designfiles, postindustria) appear every month and none mention Patina. The target list should now also include remodelai.io, 92interiors.com, and decorviz.ai — all new, small, and likely reachable.

4. **Entity/on-page prerequisites remain open, with a regression.** Per the August SEO audit: live titles still doubled (`| Patina | Patina`), `og-image.jpg`/`logo.png` still 404 (third month), no FAQ/ItemList schema, no GSC property. AI-summary inclusion is unlikely while basic entity signals are broken on the live site.

5. **Query 3's field rotation cuts both ways.** With the summary tilted toward vintage/resale marketplaces, a "designer-curated furniture *platforms*" post that explicitly contrasts curation-of-new (Patina's model) against curation-of-vintage (Pamono/1stDibs/Chairish) would be differentiated against everything currently cited — but only if published.
