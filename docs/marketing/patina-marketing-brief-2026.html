Executive verdict02 / 22
The short version

Patina looks considered. It does not yet look earned.

The strategy is better than the evidence surrounding it. The handoff from self-directed discovery to a real designer is distinctive. The website, however, still asks selective audiences to believe the model before they can see the people, work, and relationships that make it credible.

The next year is not primarily a reach problem. It is a proof-building problem followed by a focused distribution problem.

Immediate correction06 / 22
Before promotion

Six things must become true.

  1. Every future-state sentence is dated or framed as intent.
  2. Every primary CTA matches the audience track.
  1. Real designers and makers appear by name, face, practice, and point of view.
  2. The live portal is shown—not merely promised.
  1. Owned imagery replaces generic category atmosphere.
  2. The publishing calendar becomes an operating commitment.

Promotion before these corrections would scale skepticism, not respect.

Priority audience / designers08 / 22
They are the authority

Respect starts by giving up the hero role.

A selective designer does not need another brand telling them the industry is broken. They need evidence that Patina understands the practice and will strengthen—not flatten—their expertise.

They must hear

  • You shape the vocabulary.
  • You receive a running start.
  • Your expertise is named.
  • The client relationship remains yours.

They must see

  • A working portal.
  • A real practice behind it.
  • Clear ownership and economics.
  • Other credible practitioners participating.

Primary CTA: “Apply to the Founding 50.” Applications are read personally, with a documented seven-day response expectation.

Proof architecture16 / 22
How respect is earned

Build proof in four layers.

I

People

  • Founders in their real work
  • Named designer council
  • Named maker partners
  • Credentials and project context
II

Practice

  • Portal walkthrough
  • Taxonomy working session
  • Real intake artifact
  • Before/after workflow
III

Objects

  • Product record
  • Material rationale
  • Construction detail
  • Maker provenance
IV

Outcomes

  • Qualified introductions
  • Time saved
  • Selections understood
  • Published partner stories

Until outcome proof exists, lead with people, practice, and objects. Do not manufacture the fourth layer.

Content authority engine19 / 22
A sustainable cadence

One interview should do four jobs.

01 / Capture

One 60-minute expert conversation

Leah, a founding designer, or a maker speaks from active practice.

02 / Publish

One definitive article

Answer a real question with specifics, objects, and documented judgment.

03 / Distribute

Newsletter + three field notes

Translate the argument for email, LinkedIn, Instagram, and direct outreach.

04 / Compound

Pitch + reference asset
