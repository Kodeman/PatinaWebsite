# Patina — photography shot list

Photography will enrich the redesign over time. A complete shoot is not a launch dependency. The first release should lead with rooms, materials, and the making; founder visibility stays low.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## What to capture alongside the images

- Landscape and portrait compositions for the major scenes; allow room around important objects for responsive cropping.
- A clean version of each composition, without headline text, graphic overlays, or filters baked in.
- For every documentary image: who or what is pictured, where it was made, photographer credit, and a short confirmed detail we can tell the visitor.
- For a connected piece story: the actual piece, its maker, its materials, and which process the workshop photograph shows. Unrelated room and workshop photographs should not imply one product's provenance.

## Art direction

Warm, natural, and used with care. Show evidence of decisions and work. Keep personal objects specific to the real people and place. The website's motion should carry the transition between the photographs; the images themselves do not need theatrical staging.

Founder photography can stay on Our Story and selected letters until Kody and Leah want a more visible role on the homepage. Retain the Patina Strata in the site's identity; do not bake it into the photography.

## Launch before the shoot

Use the approved visual direction with an image-by-image launch review of the assets already available. Keep illustrative study imagery distinguishable from documented projects and maker stories. Build stable image placements and responsive crops so real photography can be introduced progressively.
