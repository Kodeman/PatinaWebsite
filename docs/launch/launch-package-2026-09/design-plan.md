# Launch-book design plan

This is a working studio folio for the two founders. It should feel related to the Living Study and make the launch easy to assess together. It is not a new public landing page.

- Palette: warm paper `#FAF7F2`, off-white `#EDE9E4`, clay `#A3927C`, mocha `#655B52`, charcoal `#3F3B37`, deep reading ink `#2C2926`. Existing campaign artwork supplies its approved secondary colors.
- Typography: Libre Caslon Display for short, quiet chapter statements; Manrope for instructions and tables. Local font files travel with the package.
- Structure: a landscape folio with twelve chapters; broad reading margins, an actual letter, a six-week plan, and the finished asset contact sheet. Fixed chapter pages for PDF; responsive reflow for a phone.
- Identity: reuse the exact compact Strata SVG produced from the existing component. Do not draw a new mark or stretch it non-uniformly.
- Distinctive gesture: a cover that combines a spacious invitation with the chair study; the following pages become practical working spreads. The campaign imagery carries the craft context; avoid decorative charts or invented material samples.
- Motion: no autoplay. Navigation is direct, keyboard-visible, and respects reduced motion. The separate motion storyboard carries the studio-walkthrough direction for campaign production.

Review against brief: a cream/serif treatment is justified by Patina’s existing identity, not selected as a generic premium template. The book uses that identity to present concrete people, correspondence, assets, and dates-relative-to-action. Numbers label actual steps and weeks. No invented results, portraits, quotes, or case studies.
