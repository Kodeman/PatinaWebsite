# Patina — twelve launch posts

Prepared 11 September 2026. Final draft copy for founder review; no post has been published or scheduled. Instagram and LinkedIn are the focus. Consumer membership leads; two dedicated posts support the designer and maker invitations. These posts follow the [messaging system](../../../messaging/patina-messaging-system.md) and [production direction](../../../../design-proposals/living-study/PRODUCTION-BRIEF.md).

Treat the numbered posts as a content library, not twelve consecutive sales announcements. Use the launch calendar to give each idea room. First-person posts are proposed founder language, not verbatim quotations. All app and marketplace references preserve the pre-launch state.

The creative references C01–C06 point to the companion creative package. Reusing a finished card is the ready option; optional extra story frames and video edits are production briefs. Preserve the approved Strata. Where study imagery is used, keep **“Illustrative imagery — awaiting partner photography.”** visible on the asset and in its caption. Do not present a pictured chair as available, or a pictured workshop as a signed partner.

Use the full attributed destination for the actual clickable placement. For Instagram, pair the feed post with a story using that destination; the caption gives the short readable address. Do not promise “link in bio” unless the correct destination is actually set. A shared profile link should receive its own generic profile attribution rather than pretending to identify every individual feed post.

## Community replies

Use a human reply when someone asks a real question; direct private application or membership details to hello@patina.cloud. The [FAQ and interview drafts](founder-narrative-and-faq.md) supply grounded answers. Do not paste a membership pitch under every comment or treat a like as permission for private outreach.

If someone asks whether they can buy or download now: “The app and marketplace are still pre-launch. The website introduces what we’re building, and the Founding Circle is a way to help shape it, with first access when the app launches.”

If someone asks about a pictured piece: “This image is an illustrative study for the website. It isn’t a listing for an available piece. We’re adding partner photography as those stories are ready.” For a real Middle West Studio image, identify the actual project and studio instead.

If someone shares a personal story, thank them for the specific detail they chose to share. Seek a separate affirmative choice before reusing their words, name, project, or image in another post. Avoid reposting screenshots of private replies.
