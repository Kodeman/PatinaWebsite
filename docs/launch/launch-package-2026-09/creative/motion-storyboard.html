Patina — a walk through the studio

Format: 20-second vertical reel, 1080 × 1920. Purpose: introduce Patina through the approved room → study → making experience, then invite a Founding Circle signup. Audience: people who care about furniture that belongs in their home. CTA: Join the Founding Circle.

An interactive composition is supplied in motion-preview.html. It plays only when requested, pauses, scrubs, respects reduced motion, and stops on the final invitation. It is a reviewable motion master, not an encoded MP4. The supplied C03 PNG is ready to use as a static alternative. No audio or stock music has been purchased, generated, or licensed for this package.

Accessibility and a static counterpart

  • Ready static asset: exports/c03-studio-story.png, 1080 × 1920. It carries the central message, source label, CTA, and pre-launch context without motion.
  • Image description: “Patina invites you to help shape what belongs. An illustrative room and isolated chair study introduce the Living Study. Illustrative imagery — awaiting partner photography. Join the Founding Circle at patina.cloud/founding for first access when the app launches.”
  • No audio dependency: the full argument and action are in the visible type. The preview has no audio track. If the team adds narration, supply accurate captions and keep the written CTA.
  • Reduced-motion preview: stationary images with direct changes between still scenes. The manual timeline exposes each scene without requiring playback.
  • Timing: no flashing, quick zooms, rotating camera, or timed interaction. The final invitation stays available after playback ends.

Production notes for the editor

Use the included local fonts and their licenses. Preserve the exact Strata geometry, independent of the image plane. Build a 1080 × 1920 timeline at a constant frame rate supported by the editor; 30 fps is the working export target. Use the preview for composition and timing, not a screen capture of its surrounding controls.

The composition reserves breathing room at the top and bottom. Keep the final CTA and URL clear in the publishing preview; if app controls overlap them, move the full invitation group upward together. Do not crop off the illustrative caption to make room. Export the final deliverable as an ordinary video file from the editor; confirm the total duration is 20 seconds and the final frame remains legible.
