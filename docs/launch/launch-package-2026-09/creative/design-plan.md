# Creative direction: an invitation into the work

Prepared 11 September 2026. Campaign artwork extends the approved Living Study; it does not introduce a separate identity.

Libre Caslon Display carries generous, conversational headlines; Manrope carries the useful details. The existing Georgia, tracked-uppercase wordmark remains consistent with the production navigation. The compact, left-aligned Strata geometry is copied without changing ratios or colors.

## Composition

The creative system has three physical references: an invitation laid on a studio table, a room study with its caption attached, and a working note exchanged between peers. Each asset uses one dominant gesture and a clear action. All copy is left-aligned except the centered invitation card. The Strata stays distinct from photographs.

## Review against the brief

The cream-and-serif palette is retained because it is Patina’s approved production design. To keep this from becoming a generic cream campaign, artwork borrows specific compositions from the Living Study: the folded invitation, room-to-study sequence, and maker note. There are no decorative statistics, fabricated handwriting, staged founder portraits, or improvised material provenance. Image-free pieces can be published before photography, while illustrated studies retain a visible source caption. CTA labels match the live audience paths exactly.

The campaign headline is a headline, not a replacement tagline. The only signature line is **Where Time Adds Value.**
