Use DESIGN CHICAGO for a design-practice inquiry, and Midwest Design for a craft question with a Minnesota reporting perspective. Story boundaries that preserve credibility

  • “Designer-taught” is the intended product approach. Do not write as if a released app already proves a measurable performance improvement.
  • The 200 / 50 / 15 figures are program sizes, not traction. A dashboard screenshot or a small observed cohort is not a public enrollment claim without reconciliation.
  • Founder biographies are factual editorial summaries. First-person pitches are drafts for the sender; no invented quotations have been attributed to either founder.
  • Middle West Studio work documents Leah’s practice. It is not a Patina case study, supplier roster, marketplace listing, or local Chicago/Minnesota commission.
  • Photography is a follow-on opportunity, not a prerequisite for announcing the website. The owner’s permission for website use does not by itself establish every photographer’s press-republication license.
  • No public product launch date, embargo, media partnership, paid placement, or event is booked. Omit stale award deadlines and advertising calendars from the earned plan.

Assets to offer in a real conversation

Start with the background sheet and the public site. Offer a short founder interview, a screen walk-through that distinguishes website from future product, and one carefully credited image relevant to the story. Supply original project photography for reuse only when the particular permission and publication-history questions have been answered. A journalist may prefer their own photographs or reporting; do not promise final-copy approval as a condition of participating.

For a second-wave craft feature, use an actual maker’s account of their work with their permission. For a designer-practice feature, offer the professional’s own observations. Neither requires naming them a Patina partner if the relationship is still a conversation. Kody can own those introductions, while Leah checks that design and project details remain accurate.
