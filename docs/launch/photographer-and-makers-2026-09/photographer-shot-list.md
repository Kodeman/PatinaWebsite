## 06 / 05 / Capture and delivery

Make every frame useful.

Light & finish

Warmth should come from the light and materials. Keep whites neutral, timber believable and shadow detail present. Retouch distractions with care; retain grain, wear and signs of use. No text or brand marks baked into photographs.

Continuity & cropping

For S01, S03 and S06, record lens, camera height, exposure and piece position. Review a rough room-to-piece alignment before moving anything. Compose landscape and portrait separately; preserve the full silhouette and generous edges.

Stills delivery

Proposed quote target: 20-24 edited full-resolution selects covering the priority shots; final count agreed with photographer. Deliver sRGB JPEG masters, preferably 5,000+ pixels on the long edge when source capture allows. Supply 16:9, 4:5 and 9:16 crops for S01-S03/S16 where composed.

Motion & cutout options

If commissioned: 4K clips where available, a consistent 24 or 30 fps, no titles or baked-in music, plus camera originals. For S03, include a transparent PNG cutout only if extraction is in scope; otherwise supply the clean, full-resolution source and shadow plate.

Captions, credit & usage

For every select, record photographer, date, location, people, actual piece/maker, materials and a confirmed detail. Agree website, organic social, email and press use; specify paid use separately if planned. Record credits and any agreed restrictions alongside filenames.

Delivery folder: 01_Masters / 02_Web_Crops / 03_Motion / 04_Captions
