Draft copy for a coordinated website and Founding Circle launch. Publish only after the linked destinations are live and checked. The app and marketplace remain pre-launch. Membership is open everywhere; the initial outreach focus is Chicago–Madison–Minneapolis.

**Supporting copy:** We’re building Patina to put a great designer’s eye within reach. Join the Founding Circle to help shape a more personal way to discover furniture that belongs.

**Primary CTA:** Join the Founding Circle

## Copy handling

- Use a single primary CTA on each asset. Designer and maker assets get their own CTA rather than three competing buttons.
- Use the approved platform preview after the site is live; do not imply that a concept furniture image is an item for sale.
- Current enrollment is not supplied. Avoid countdowns, remaining-place counts, acceptance counts, or language suggesting the circle has already filled.
- Keep the member invitation distinct from future design-service availability. Worldwide membership is not a promise of worldwide service delivery.
