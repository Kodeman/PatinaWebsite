## Short description

Patina is developing a designer-taught furniture platform in Madison, Wisconsin. Founded by Leah and Kody Kochaver, it brings a designer’s judgment, furniture with a story, and the creative process into one experience. The app and marketplace are pre-launch; its Founding Circle invites people to help shape what comes next.

## Founder bios

**Leah Kochaver — Co-Founder, Design & Experience**

Leah Kochaver leads Design & Experience at Patina and runs Middlewest Studio, her interior-design practice. Her work informs Patina’s point of view: the value lies in a designer’s judgment about what belongs in a room. She is building Patina with Kody Kochaver to bring that judgment within reach and give people a path to deeper design guidance.

**Kody Kochaver — Co-Founder, Technology & Product**

Kody Kochaver leads Technology & Product at Patina. He is building the platform in response to a gap he sees in existing tools, which can feel sterile and disconnected from creative work. His focus is making the tools feel like part of the process, working alongside Leah’s design perspective and the people who will help shape Patina.

**Shared bio**

Leah and Kody Kochaver founded Patina in Madison, Wisconsin, in 2023. Leah brings the perspective of her interior-design practice, Middlewest Studio; Kody leads the technology and product. Together, they are developing a furniture platform that connects a designer’s eye with a more personal way to discover what belongs in a home.
