# Madison-founded Patina introduces its Living Study website and invites a 200-member Founding Circle

**Leah and Kody Kochaver are building a designer-taught approach to furniture discovery, with early community outreach from Chicago through Madison to Minneapolis.**

**MADISON, Wisconsin — [RELEASE DATE]** — Patina, founded in Madison by Leah and Kody Kochaver, has introduced a redesigned website and an invitation to join its Founding Circle. The community of up to 200 members will help shape a furniture platform built around the judgment of working interior designers, the people behind the furniture, and materials that gain character with use.

The website’s Living Study moves visitors through a room, a material study, and the making process. Its warm visual language and connected motion introduce the ideas behind Patina before inviting visitors into the founding community. A complete reduced-motion experience is available. The Patina Strata remains part of the brand’s identity.

Leah leads Design & Experience and runs Middlewest Studio, her interior-design practice. Her work informs Patina’s ambition to make a designer’s eye more accessible. Kody leads Technology & Product. His motivation comes from a gap he sees in existing tools: they can feel sterile and disconnected from the creative process. Together, the founders are building tools intended to feel at home in that process.

Patina’s app and marketplace remain pre-launch. The planned experience will help people discover furniture that belongs in their space and continue with a designer when they want more guidance. The website introduces that direction; it does not offer marketplace purchasing or a public app download.

Founding Circle members receive updates from the founders, monthly opportunities to influence product decisions, first access when the app launches, and permanent founding status. Members can also choose recognition at launch. The 200-member figure is the program’s cap, not a statement of remaining availability.

Patina’s first invitations will grow from Leah’s client relationships and Madison-area outreach, with a regional focus on the Chicago–Madison–Minneapolis corridor. Founding Circle membership has no geographic restriction.

Working designers and furniture makers also have dedicated invitations. The Founding 50 invites designers to help shape the platform. Patina is seeking 15 Founding Makers whose work reflects its emphasis on craft over volume. Maker placement is based on craft, with no sponsored placements or pay-to-play.

Explore the Living Study and join the Founding Circle at [patina.cloud](https://patina.cloud). Designers can learn more at [patina.cloud/designers](https://patina.cloud/designers), and makers at [patina.cloud/makers](https://patina.cloud/makers).

**About Patina**

Founded in 2023 by Leah and Kody Kochaver in Madison, Wisconsin, Patina is developing a designer-taught furniture platform. It aims to put a great designer’s eye within reach and create a path from personal discovery to a full designer engagement. Its app and marketplace are pre-launch. Where Time Adds Value.
