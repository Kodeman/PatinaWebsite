The consumer-app sentence needs to go whatever else is decided: it promises a product VISION no longer describes.

05 · Proposal · Moll Drive house

Credit the house. Don't claim it.

We can say

  • Middle West Studio designed the interiors of the Moll Drive house in Waunakee (C28)
  • Kippley Custom Carpentry is a family-owned builder in Sauk City, building since 1987 (C25)
  • Madison Fall Parade of Homes, Oct 9–11 and 16–18 (C27)

We can't

  • Say or imply that Kippley or the house uses Patina
  • Show a photo without Kippley's written OK, one photo at a time

Open question

Most Parade visitors are homeowners, and every Parade CTA asks for studios. Should homeowners get a soft ask, the newsletter (The Designer's Eye), on the page /parade lands on?

Sources: claims.md C25–C29; docs/marketing/partners/kippley.md

We propose a credit line on /about, or a short journal note, that names Middle West Studio's role in the house. It makes no product claim. The open question is the one the Parade raises: most people who walk through are homeowners, and the printed pieces only ask studios to act. A newsletter ask for homeowners costs little, but it changes what /designers is for, so it's a founders' decision.

07 · Measurement

30

pageviews recorded in the last 30 days. The last one was Sep 26.

Consent gate. PostHog recorded nothing until a visitor clicked Accept, so the site was effectively unmeasured.

Double counting. The browser and the server both sent each signup under the same name. The one Founding Circle signup shows as two.

Clicks went untracked. Autocapture was off and 17 defined events were never sent.

No one was told. Signups landed in Supabase without an email to anyone.

Source: PostHog project 326191, queried Oct 9; src/lib/posthog.ts, src/lib/analytics.ts

The site is live and the PostHog proxy works. The problem was configuration: the client opted out of capture by default until consent was granted. Almost nobody clicks Accept, so the October campaigns would have run with no way to see whether they worked. That has now changed; the next slide shows how.

08 · Built now, no copy changes

Every visit counted, every signup emailed.

Counted without cookies

Before consent, PostHog counts visits anonymously with no cookies. Accept upgrades to a full profile. Clicks, scroll depth and dead clicks are captured, and each signup is counted once.

An email to kody@ for every signup

Founding Circle, newsletter, designer, maker and contact. Each email shows who it was, where they came from (campaign, channel), and a link to their PostHog profile.

A daily report at 8:00

Emailed to kody@patina.cloud: yesterday's visitors, campaign traffic, signups by type, and the /designers to Founding 50 funnel.

A test that guards it

An automated check visits every page and clicks every link and button. It fails if anything goes untracked.

Live after the next deploy · PostHog dashboard 2084092, subscription 167653

All of this is plumbing. It changes no customer-facing copy, which was the agreed boundary. The daily report and dashboard already exist in PostHog. The code changes go live with the next deploy, which Kody approves. After the deploy, we send a real test signup to confirm the alert reaches kody@. The report's time is 8:00 Central during daylight time, and 7:00 after Nov 1, because PostHog schedules in UTC.
