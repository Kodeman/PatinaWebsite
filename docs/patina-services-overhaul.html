Designer Services
Page Overhaul

The current page sells fixed packages from a fictional in-house team. The real model is different — and better. This deck walks through what's wrong, what should change, and exactly what the new page says.

02 The Audit — What's Wrong

The current page sells a product
that doesn't exist

Four structural problems make the page incompatible with Patina's actual business model.

Problem 01

Fake Price Tiers

Three rigid packages — Free / $450 / Custom — present Patina as a commoditized design service. This is a SaaS pricing page for something that isn't SaaS.

Current: "Room Design — $450 — 5-7 furniture recommendations, 3 revision rounds"
Problem 02

Fictional In-House Team

Copy says "our design team" and "our designers." Patina doesn't employ designers — it matches you with independent firms. This sets a false expectation from the first sentence.

Current: "Our design team is here to guide you through our collection"
Problem 03

The App Advantage Is Invisible

The page mentions "AR preview demo" as a bullet point. The massive competitive advantage — that your designer receives your style profile, room scans, saved products, and behavioral signals before they ever meet you — is completely absent.

Problem 04

Generic "How It Works"

Discovery → Curation → Visualization → Delivery. Any design platform could put this on their page. Nothing here communicates what makes Patina different from Havenly, Decorilla, or a local designer's website.

03 Strategic Reframe

From price menu
to matchmaking

The page needs to communicate three things the current version doesn't:

Current Positioning

Patina is the design firm.

Fixed packages. Standardized deliverables. "Our team" does the work. You pick a tier and pay a price. Technology is a line item in a bullet list.

→
New Positioning

Patina is the bridge.

You're matched with a real designer at a real firm who already understands your space, your taste, and your constraints — because the app told them. They craft a custom proposal. No tiers, no fixed prices.

Three truths the new page must land:
1. You're connecting with a real, independent design firm — not a Patina employee.
2. The app captures enough intelligence to give your designer a massive head start.
3. Every project gets a custom proposal. No tiers. No cookie cutters.

A Single Room

Focused guidance for one space — a living room that needs direction, a bedroom that's never felt finished, a home office that actually works.

When your living room, dining area, and kitchen need to feel like one cohesive story. Your designer coordinates flow, palette, and proportion across the whole space.

Comprehensive design across multiple rooms. Your designer builds a vision for how every space relates — from the entryway that sets the tone to the bedroom that closes the day.

05 What Changed — Summary

What we killed,
what we built

Killed

Three-Tier Pricing Grid

Free / $450 / Custom tiers are gone. They contradict the custom-proposal model and commoditize the designer relationship. Replaced with "What to Expect" scoping examples.

Built

The Briefing Package

New Section 02 shows exactly what data the app captures and how it reaches the designer. This is the competitive moat — now it's visible.

Killed

"Our Design Team" Language

Every reference to an in-house team is replaced with "independent designers," "your designer," and "real firms." Patina is the bridge, not the firm.

Built

Designer Match Card

New Section 04 shows a real match preview with style compatibility score. Makes the matching algorithm tangible and the designer human.

Killed

Generic 4-Step Process

Discovery → Curation → Visualization → Delivery was interchangeable with any competitor. Replaced with app-first flow that highlights the data advantage.

Built

Dual CTA Strategy

"Download the App" for new users, "Connect Now" for users who already have a profile. The current page has a single dead-end mailto link.

06 Implementation Notes

Build guidance

Routes
· "Find Your Designer" → /services/match (new — triggers matching flow or app download prompt)
· "Download the App" → App Store link or /app
· "Connect Now" → /services/connect (authenticated flow for users with existing style profiles)

Sanity CMS Fields
· Hero: eyebrow, headline, sub, cta_label, cta_link
· Briefing Package: section_title, section_sub, data_items[] (icon, label, description)
· Process Steps: steps[] (number, title, body)
· Match Preview: editable example data or pull from real designer profiles (Phase 2+)
· Scoping Examples: examples[] (title, body) — repeatable array, start with 3
· Closing CTA: headline, sub, primary_cta, secondary_cta

Design Notes
· Briefing Package section uses Pearl (#F5F2EE) background for visual break
· Match Card component should become reusable — it appears in the iOS app lead flow too
· Process step connector line uses IntersectionObserver to draw left→right on scroll
· Closing section uses Charcoal background — same pattern as homepage footer CTA
· Designer quote is placeholder; replace with real testimonial before launch
· The Leah Kirkland match card is an illustrative example for the spec; production version should use a generic or rotate through real designer profiles

What This Page Does NOT Handle
· Pricing details — those live in the custom proposal, not on a marketing page
· Designer portfolio browsing — that's a future /designers/[slug] feature
· Scheduling — handled in the matching/connect flow, not the services overview
· The actual matching algorithm — this page communicates it; the app executes it
