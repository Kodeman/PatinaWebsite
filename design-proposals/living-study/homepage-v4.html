Leah / Design & Experience

It starts with
the way a designer sees.

Leah runs Middlewest Studio in Madison. Sourcing furniture for her clients is part of the work that gave Patina its starting point: the relationships between a room, its materials, and the person who lives there.

“The tools never matched how designers actually think.”

From Leah’s existing Founding Circle welcome letter.

Patina is being built to put that designer’s eye within reach, and to make it easier to continue with a working designer when you want more help.

Visit Middlewest Studio
Kody / Technology & Product

Making space
for the creative part.

Kody sees a gap in the tools available. Too many feel sterile, disconnected from the creative process they’re supposed to support.

He’s building Patina because the tools should feel like part of that process: a place to explore, notice a connection, and let an idea take shape.

That belief is shaping the experience alongside Leah’s design judgment. A room to look around. Materials to consider together. A person to continue the conversation.

We’re still
putting it together.

The app and marketplace are pre-launch. The Founding Circle is an invitation to help shape what they become, with a first letter from Leah and first access when the app launches.

About this study & its imagery

Round 04 of The Living Study, September 9, 2026. A local design prototype. The room, chair, workshop, and material images are generated concepts. They do not document one product’s maker or production process. Materials are illustrative palette choices, not available product finishes. The isolated chair is a generated interpretation of the chair in the room image.

Founder facts follow the Patina Messaging System. Kody’s motivation comes from his September 9 feedback; the story wording is an editorial draft. Leah’s quote comes from the existing Founding Circle welcome email. Founder imagery and personal origin anecdotes have not been invented.

Libre Caslon Display and Manrope / SIL Open Font License. All image prompts and source assets are retained with the presentation. This prototype does not save preferences, submit forms, customize products, or create room briefs.
